Evidence

The two caveats, and where the +67% figure actually comes from.

The Γ “100% exceedance” statistic is vacuous

The same per-run ratio, applied to unperturbed pairs, also fires 100% of the time. It cannot tell an attack from a healthy run.

+67% vs +33% is a denominator effect

Perturbed absolute error is ≈4.4 m in every configuration. Lowering Qvel tightens the nominal filter, so the same degradation reads as a bigger percentage.
